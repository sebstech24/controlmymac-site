Launcher + window switching

Open apps and jump between windows from the iPhone or iPad — a physical Stage Manager that doesn't steal screen pixels or a ⌘-tab slot.

A Grid for every Mac app

Bring Final Cut Pro, Premiere Pro or Chrome to the front and tap “Grid for …” at the top of the Grid: that app gets its own Grid, starting as a copy of your Default. From then on it switches in by itself whenever the app is in front. Want the grid you pick to stay put? Flip Auto-Switch off, right in the Grid. Learn combos from the Mac or set them by hand, and put Grid buttons and your own shortcuts in the trackpad's top row, up to 12 at a time.

Latency you won't notice

Input goes straight from iPhone or iPad to Mac over your local network or Bluetooth. No relay server, no internet dependency — your keystrokes never leave the room.

Nothing phoning home

No account, no servers, no ads. The connection is encrypted device-to-device, paired by scanning a QR code — not by opening firewall ports or typing IP addresses.

All your grids in one place

Tap the grid's name to see every grid and the apps it's for. Manage Grids, also in Settings under Grids, lets you rename a grid, pick which Mac apps use it, set its rows and columns, or delete it. How App Grids Work explains it all in the app.

Dark mode, your accent, help on tap

Settings → Look: System, Light or Dark, plus an accent color from seven presets or any color you like. Dark mode is warm and low-glare for late sessions. Help Mode makes every button explain itself until you turn it off. All free.
