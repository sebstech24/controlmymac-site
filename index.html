Before you buy

Can I use my iPhone as a mouse for my Mac without an app?

No. As of September 2026, macOS has no built-in way to use an iPhone as a mouse or trackpad. Universal Control shares a Mac's keyboard and trackpad with a nearby Mac or iPad, and Sidecar turns an iPad into a second display. Neither supports iPhone.

Does it need Wi-Fi?

No. It works over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi. Bluetooth gives the smoothest pointer and works without any network. On Wi-Fi, both devices need to be on the same network, and guest or hotel networks may block devices from seeing each other.

Do I need an account?

No. There's nothing to sign up for, on Essentials or with Full App. You pair by scanning a QR code on your Mac.

Which Macs and iPhones work?

The Mac app needs macOS 14 or later on an Apple silicon Mac. Intel Macs are supported up to version 1.2.3. The iPhone and iPad app needs iOS or iPadOS 18 or later.

Does it work with any app on my Mac?

It moves your Mac's pointer and types into whatever has focus, key by key, like a real mouse and keyboard. To do that, the Mac app needs the macOS Accessibility permission, which it asks for once.

Can each Mac app have its own Grid?

Yes, with Full App. Bring the app to the front on your Mac, then tap “Grid for …” at the top of the Grid (it shows that app's name). The app gets its own Grid, starting as a copy of your Default grid, and from then on it switches in by itself whenever that app is in front. Every other app uses Default. Tap the grid's name to see all your grids and the apps they're for, or turn Auto-Switch off to keep the grid you pick on screen.

Is there a dark mode?

Yes, and it's free for everyone. Open Settings, then Look, to choose System, Light or Dark and an accent color from seven presets or any color you like. Dark mode uses warm, low-glare colors that are easy on the eyes.

Is the connection private?

Yes. No account, no servers, no analytics. Your iPhone or iPad and Mac talk directly over your local network or Bluetooth, encrypted. The camera is used only to scan the pairing QR code.

What's free and what's in Full App?

The Mac app is free. In the iPhone and iPad app, Essentials is free forever: trackpad movement, click and right click, Live and Draft Keyboard basics, one Grid button and Launcher preview, plus dark mode, accent colors and Help Mode. Full App adds scrolling and Mini Trackpad controls, the full Grid including a Grid for every Mac app with Auto-Switch, Launcher and window switching without upgrade prompts, Mac shortcuts including your own shortcut buttons, gestures, presets and tuning. It's $3.99/month after a free 7-day trial, or $17.99 once, in the US (€4.99 or €19.99 in euro countries), bought in the iPhone or iPad app through Apple. Neither plan has ads.
