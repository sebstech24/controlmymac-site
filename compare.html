Where Control My Mac is different

It only does Mac, on purpose, and it spends that focus on a few things.

A Grid for every Mac app

Give any Mac app its own Grid of shortcut buttons in one tap. It switches in by itself when that app comes forward, and one menu shows all your grids and the apps they're for. Record shortcuts straight from the Mac's keyboard instead of typing them in. More in the Stream Deck alternative guide.

One finger, end to end

Pointer, click, typing, shortcuts and even pairing work with one hand. Two-key shortcuts become single taps. See the one-handed guide.

Write first, then send

The Draft keyboard lets you write and edit a whole message on the phone, then insert it on the Mac in one go. No racing a live cursor.

Private and ad-free

No account, no servers, no ads in any plan. Your devices talk directly to each other, encrypted. The privacy policy is short and specific.

When another app is better

Straight answers, even when the answer isn't us.

You use Windows or Linux

Control My Mac only controls Macs. Remote Mouse and Unified Remote cover Windows and Linux, and Mobile Mouse covers Windows.

Your phone is Android

Control My Mac is iPhone and iPad only. Remote Mouse and Unified Remote both have Android apps.

You want a remote made for each app

Unified Remote ships with 100+ remotes built for specific programs, and Remote Mouse and Mobile Mouse offer media remotes. Control My Mac gives you a general trackpad and keyboard, plus a Grid for each app that you fill with the shortcuts you use.

You want it on your wrist

Mobile Mouse and Remote Mouse have Apple Watch apps. Control My Mac doesn't.

You want to wake or shut down the computer

Unified Remote lists Wake-on-LAN and power controls, and Remote Mouse lists power options. We don't offer power controls.

You want to pay once and pay little

Mobile Mouse is $9.99 up front, and Unified Remote's full version is $4.99. Control My Mac's lifetime option is $17.99, or you can stay on the free Essentials plan.
