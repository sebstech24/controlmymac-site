Privacy Policy

Control My Mac is an iPhone & iPad app and a free Mac menu-bar companion that let your iPhone or iPad act as a trackpad, keyboard and remote control for your Mac.

Effective date: 26 September 2026

Controller: Sebastian Apps (developer of Control My Mac)

Address: Šturmova ulica 7A, Ljubljana, Slovenia

Contact: support@controlmymac.com

Summary

Your remote-control activity stays between your devices. There is no Control My Mac account. Your iPhone or iPad and your Mac talk directly over your local network or Bluetooth, and that connection is encrypted. Optional email and update-notification features use a small amount of data only when you choose to use them.

Core app activity

  • No account or sign-in. The apps work without a profile or registration.
  • No advertising or behavioral tracking. The apps do not contain advertising SDKs and do not track what you do on your Mac.
  • Direct device control. Pointer movements, taps, scrolls, keystrokes you choose to send, app-launch commands and window-switch commands travel directly from your iPhone or iPad to your paired Mac. They are encrypted, used only to control your Mac in real time, and are never sent to us or to a third party.

Free-month gift and Sebastian Apps emails

The free month of Full App is a welcome gift for people who join Sebastian Apps emails, so it is only available if you agree to receive them. When you request it, we collect your email address, language, a record of your consent (when you gave it and which wording you agreed to), delivery and unsubscribe status, and the Apple offer code assigned to you. The email address and code are encrypted in our database. To prevent abuse and to give each address the gift only once, we also keep keyed hashes of your email address and IP address.

We use this to send you the gift email and, after that, occasional news and promo codes for Control My Mac and other Sebastian Apps. Vercel runs the form, Neon stores the encrypted record, Brevo sends the emails and keeps the mailing list, and Cloudflare Turnstile checks that the request comes from a person. Apple processes the code when you redeem it.

  • Legal basis. Your consent (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR) for the emails. The abuse checks (the hashes and the security check) rely on our legitimate interest in handing out the gift fairly (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Withdrawing consent. You can withdraw your consent at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email or by writing to us. This does not affect emails sent before, and a code you already received stays valid.
  • How long we keep it. We keep your record while you are subscribed. When you unsubscribe, your address is removed from the mailing list and we keep only the encrypted record of your request, so that your unsubscribe is respected and the address cannot claim the gift again. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Optional update notifications

If you turn on notifications in the iPhone or iPad app, Apple gives the app an opaque device token. We store that token encrypted with a random app-installation identifier, language, app version and delivery status. We do not attach it to your email address or Apple Account. It is used only for short summaries of Control My Mac features, improvements and important app updates. Apple Push Notification service delivers the notification. Turning notifications off in system Settings stops alerts; you may also contact us to request deletion.

Website measurement

The website uses Vercel Web Analytics to understand aggregate visits and page performance. It is not used for advertising or to build personal profiles.

Device permissions

The apps request only the permissions needed to function:

  • Local Network — to discover and connect to your Mac on your own Wi-Fi network.
  • Bluetooth — as an alternative, internet-free connection method.
  • Camera (iPhone or iPad) — used for exactly one thing: scanning the pairing QR code shown on your Mac. No photos or video are captured, stored or transmitted.
  • Notifications (iPhone or iPad) — optional. Used for short summaries of new features, improvements and important app updates.
  • Accessibility (Mac) — so the Mac companion can move the pointer, click and type on your behalf. Used solely to carry out the actions you send from your iPhone or iPad.

You can revoke any of these permissions in your device settings at any time.

Purchases

The Mac app is free. The iPhone & iPad app can start a free 7-day trial, continue with the free Essentials Plan, or unlock Full App through monthly or lifetime in-app purchases processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details; purchase and subscription management is handled by Apple under Apple's privacy policy. Paid upgrades exist only in the iPhone & iPad app and are bought there through Apple — you can't pay on this website or in the free Mac app.

Children

The apps and optional email signup are not designed for children under 13.

Your rights

You can ask for access to, correction or deletion of the email or notification records we hold, ask us to restrict their use, receive them in a portable format, or object to their use, by writing to support@controlmymac.com. You can withdraw your email consent at any time, as described above. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU or EEA country where you live or work; in the UK, that is the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). Data handled by Apple, such as purchase records and push delivery, is also governed by Apple's policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the effective date above will be updated and material changes will be highlighted on this page before they take effect.
