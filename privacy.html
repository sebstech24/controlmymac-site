Privacy Policy

Control My Mac is an iPhone & iPad app and a free Mac menu-bar companion that turn your iPhone or iPad into a trackpad, keyboard and remote control for your Mac.

Effective date: 26 September 2026

Controller: Sebastian Apps (developer of Control My Mac)

Address: Šturmova ulica 7A, Ljubljana, Slovenia

Contact: support@controlmymac.com

Summary

Your remote-control activity stays between your devices. There is no Control My Mac account. Your iPhone or iPad and your Mac talk directly over your local network or Bluetooth, and that connection is encrypted. Optional email and update-notification features use a little data, and only if you choose them.

Core app activity

  • No account or sign-in. The apps work without a profile or registration.
  • No advertising or behavioral tracking. The apps contain no advertising SDKs and don’t track what you do on your Mac.
  • Direct device control. Pointer movements, taps, scrolls, the keystrokes you choose to send, and app-launch and window-switch commands go directly from your iPhone or iPad to your paired Mac. They are encrypted, used only to control your Mac in real time, and are never sent to us or to a third party.

Free-month gift and Sebastian Apps emails

The free month of Full App is a welcome gift for joining Sebastian Apps emails, so you only get it if you agree to receive them. When you request it, we collect your email address, language, a record of your consent (when you gave it and which wording you agreed to), delivery and unsubscribe status, and the Apple offer code assigned to you. The email address and code are encrypted in our database. To prevent abuse and give each address the gift only once, we also keep keyed hashes of your email and IP addresses.

We use this to send you the gift email, then occasional news and promo codes for Control My Mac and other Sebastian Apps. Vercel runs the form, Neon stores the encrypted record, Brevo sends the emails and keeps the mailing list, and Cloudflare Turnstile checks that the request comes from a person. Apple processes the code when you redeem it.

  • Legal basis. Your consent for the emails (Art. 6(1)(a) GDPR). For the abuse checks (the hashes and the security check), our legitimate interest in handing out the gift fairly (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR).
  • Withdrawing consent. You can withdraw it at any time with the unsubscribe link in every email or by writing to us. This doesn’t affect emails already sent, and a code you already received stays valid.
  • How long we keep it. We keep your record while you are subscribed. When you unsubscribe, we remove your address from the mailing list and keep only the encrypted record of your request, so your unsubscribe is respected and the address can’t claim the gift again. You can ask us to delete it at any time.

Optional update notifications

If you turn on notifications in the iPhone or iPad app, Apple gives the app an opaque device token. We store it encrypted, together with a random app-installation identifier, language, app version and delivery status, and don’t link it to your email address or Apple Account. It is used only for short summaries of Control My Mac features, improvements and important app updates, delivered by Apple Push Notification service. Turning notifications off in system Settings stops alerts; you can also ask us to delete the data.

Website measurement

The website uses Vercel Web Analytics to understand aggregate visits and page performance, not for advertising or to build personal profiles.

Device permissions

The apps request only the permissions needed to function:

  • Local Network — to discover and connect to your Mac on your own Wi-Fi network.
  • Bluetooth — as an alternative, internet-free connection method.
  • Camera (iPhone or iPad) — only to scan the pairing QR code shown on your Mac. No photos or video are captured, stored or transmitted.
  • Notifications (iPhone or iPad) — optional, for short summaries of new features, improvements and important app updates.
  • Accessibility (Mac) — so the Mac companion can move the pointer, click and type, solely to carry out the actions you send from your iPhone or iPad.

You can revoke any of these permissions in your device settings at any time.

Purchases

The Mac app is free. The iPhone & iPad app can start a free 7-day trial, continue with the free Essentials Plan, or unlock Full App through monthly or lifetime in-app purchases processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or store your payment details; Apple manages purchases and subscriptions under Apple's privacy policy. Paid upgrades exist only in the iPhone & iPad app and are bought there through Apple — you can't pay on this website or in the free Mac app.

Children

The apps and optional email signup are not designed for children under 13.

Your rights

Email support@controlmymac.com to access, correct or delete the email or notification records we hold, restrict or object to their use, or receive them in a portable format. You can withdraw your email consent at any time, as described above. You also have the right to complain to a data protection supervisory authority, in particular in the EU or EEA country where you live or work, or in the UK to the Information Commissioner’s Office (ICO). Data handled by Apple, such as purchase records and push delivery, is also governed by Apple's policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, we’ll update the effective date above and highlight material changes on this page before they take effect.
