One tap for your Mac shortcuts

Use your iPhone as a macro pad for your Mac.

A macro pad is a small board of extra keys, each one firing a shortcut you'd otherwise have to finger-twist on the keyboard. Control My Mac turns the iPhone or iPad you already own into one: a Grid of one-tap buttons for your Mac shortcuts, with its own Grid for every Mac app, switching in by itself.

Set it up in a minute

You need the free Mac app and Control My Mac on your iPhone or iPad. Pairing is one QR scan, then:

1. Open the Grid

Tap Grid at the bottom of the app. The grid you see first is Default, the one every app uses unless it has its own.

2. Add a button

Tap an empty square (+) on the Grid. Tap Start Listening, press the shortcut on your Mac's keyboard, then tap Stop Listening and Bind. Or tap Enter shortcut manually to pick the keys. A button can also send a few shortcuts back to back.

3. Name it and place it

Type a short name while recording, or touch and hold the button and tap Rename. Tap Rearrange to drag it where your thumb rests, and tap Edit Grid to pick how many rows and columns the Grid has.

4. Give busy apps their own Grid

With an app in front on your Mac, tap “Grid for” and that app's name. Its Grid switches in by itself whenever that app is in front. See the Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and Final Cut Pro guides for ready-made examples.

Good to know

What “macro” means here. Each button sends a keyboard shortcut, or a few in a row, to your Mac. It doesn't type text snippets, run scripts or wait between steps. Most things a keyboard shortcut does on your Mac, a button can do too. Media keys like volume and brightness, and shortcuts you have to hold down, aren't supported.

Questions people ask

Can an iPhone be a macro pad for a Mac?

Yes, with an app. Control My Mac turns an iPhone or iPad into a Grid of one-tap buttons that send keyboard shortcuts to your Mac over Bluetooth or Wi-Fi. You need the free Mac companion app.

Do I need to buy a hardware macro pad?

Not if your buttons are mostly keyboard shortcuts. A hardware pad is better if you want to press keys by feel without looking, or need knobs and dials.

Can the buttons change depending on the app?

Yes. Every Mac app can get its own Grid, made in one tap, that switches in by itself when that app is in front. Every other app uses your Default grid. This is part of Full App.

Does it work with Windows?

No. Control My Mac is for Macs only (macOS 14 or later), on iPhone or iPad.
