Set it up in a minute

You need the free Mac app and Control My Mac on your iPhone or iPad, paired once with a QR scan. Then:

1. Open Final Cut Pro on your Mac

Bring Final Cut Pro to the front. Control My Mac on your iPhone or iPad sees which app is in front.

2. Tap “Grid for Final Cut Pro”

In the Grid, the button names the app that's in front. One tap makes a Grid just for Final Cut Pro, starting as a copy of your Default grid.

3. Add your buttons

Tap an empty square (+) on the Grid, tap Start Listening and press the shortcut on your Mac's keyboard, then Stop Listening and Bind. Or tap Enter shortcut manually to pick the keys. Buttons copied from Default can be changed by touching and holding them (Re-record or Clear). Tap Edit Grid to add a row if you want all twelve, and Rearrange to move buttons where your thumb rests.

4. Leave Auto-Switch on

From now on the Final Cut Pro Grid appears by itself whenever Final Cut Pro is in front, and every other app uses Default. Turn Auto-Switch off if you want one Grid to stay put.

Good to know

Full App. A Grid for each Mac app and Auto-Switch are part of Full App: $3.99 a month in the US after a 7-day free trial, renewing until you cancel, or $17.99 once. The free Essentials plan includes one Grid button, so you can try the idea first.

Keyboard shortcuts only. Each button sends a key combo, or a few in a row, to your Mac. It doesn't run plugins, scripts or typed text, so anything you can't do with a keyboard shortcut in Final Cut Pro is out of reach for the Grid too.

Learning from the Mac. Recording a shortcut by pressing it on the Mac needs the Input Monitoring permission for the Mac app. It only listens while you're recording and saves just the combo. You can also set every shortcut by hand.

Your shortcuts, not ours. The list above uses Final Cut Pro's default Mac shortcuts. If you've changed yours, record them from your Mac instead and the buttons will always match.

Questions people ask

Can I control Final Cut Pro from my iPhone?

Yes, through its keyboard shortcuts. Control My Mac gives Final Cut Pro its own Grid of one-tap buttons on your iPhone or iPad that switches in whenever Final Cut Pro is in front. The same app is also a trackpad and keyboard for everything else.

Is this the same as Final Cut Pro for iPad?

No. Final Cut Pro for iPad is a separate editing app. Control My Mac is a remote for Final Cut Pro running on your Mac.

What if I changed my Final Cut Pro shortcuts?

Record each button by pressing your shortcut on the Mac, and the button sends exactly that. The table above uses Apple's defaults.

Do I need an iPad?

No. It works on iPhone. On iPad the Grid is bigger, with room for more buttons.
