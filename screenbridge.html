Questions people ask

Do I need an Apple TV to mirror my Mac to my TV?

No. With Screenbridge open on a Google TV or Android TV, the TV appears in the Mac's own Screen Mirroring list. No Apple TV or other box is needed.

Do I need to install anything on the Mac?

No. Screen Mirroring is built into macOS. Screenbridge runs on the TV only, and there is no account.

Which TVs does Screenbridge work on?

Google TV and Android TV: TVs from Sony, TCL, Hisense, Philips and others, Chromecast with Google TV, Google TV Streamer, Nvidia Shield and Android TV boxes. It has been tested on a Philips Google TV so far.

Can I use the TV as a second display?

Yes. You can mirror your Mac's screen or use the TV as a big second display.

Does it play sound on the TV?

Yes. Sound plays on the TV, and your Mac's volume keys can control the TV volume.

Why do some streaming videos show a black screen?

Protected video from services that block screen recording shows as black. This is decided by the Mac and the streaming service, not by the TV.

What does Screenbridge cost?

Start with a 7-day free trial on the Monthly plan, or buy Lifetime once and keep it. Google Play shows the price in your country. Payment and cancellation go through your Google Play account.

Does it work with iPhone and iPad?

Yes. Open Control Center, tap Screen Mirroring and choose the TV.

Is Screenbridge open source?

Yes, under GPL-3.0. The source code is on GitHub.
